# Hall window view

ImageGen created [street-at-dusk.webp](street-at-dusk.webp) as a window backdrop
for the indoor hall. The game uses a 1600×640 WebP export. Window bays sample
different horizontal portions of the same panorama.

## ImageGen prompt

```text

Show a quiet tree-lined neighborhood street running across the foreground, seen from pedestrian eye level on the opposite side of the road. A leafy row of several large deciduous trees with visible trunks stands along the sidewalk; leave clear gaps in the foliage to reveal two broad soft hills in the distance. Blue-green distant ridges under a warm peach and lavender dusk sky. Put the hill silhouettes in the upper half and the pavement and sidewalks along the lower fifth, so trees, street and hills read even through a small window. Clean illustrated shapes with gentle painted texture, readable silhouettes, subdued mint greens and indigo with a warm horizon, matching a cozy diorama. Wide 2.5:1 landscape composition, evenly interesting from left to right, single continuous landscape. The image is only the outdoor view. No window frames, wall, interior, lettering, text, signs, people, cars, logos, or watermark.
```
